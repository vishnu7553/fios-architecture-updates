# Scope and Audience

Up: [[Vision]]

> **Summary:** Who FiOS is for and what hardware it assumes.

## Audience
Developers who want a self-maintaining, private, local AI desktop and are comfortable with Linux.

## Assumptions
- A tiling Wayland desktop (Hyprland) with Quickshell. See [[Desktop Hyprland and Quickshell]].
- Enough compute for a 3B to 7B model. Exact hardware is not yet recorded: see [[Hardware Profile]].
- Arch Linux as the base. See [[Arch Base]].

## Honest limit
A 7B or smaller local model is decent at command help and summarising and unreliable at long multi-step debugging. The promised scope should be narrow. See [[Tool-Calling Reliability]].
