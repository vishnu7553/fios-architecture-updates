# FiOS

**FiOS (FRIDAY Integrated Operating System)** is a long-term plan for a minimal, security-first Arch-based distro with a local AI assistant called **FRIDAY**. It is meant to maintain the PC, run a light voice assistant, automate limited debugging, and research the web — entirely locally.

This site is the project's design vault: vision, architecture, security model, execution layer, roadmap, and decision records.
